VertiSafe

Role
Brand designer
Year
2024
Type
Rebranding
Tools
Illustrator, Photoshop, Procreate

A new name and identity for Chacanos Bolivia, the official Petzl distributor in the country, so it could speak clearly to the vertical sector.

The brand had to hold two worlds under one roof. Sport, for climbing and outdoor adventure, and Professional, for vertical work and industrial safety. The identity borrows Petzl's values of performance, innovation and technical excellence to build trust in the market.

Typeface Avenir, in Book, Medium and Black

(OFF)LINE

Role
Concept, identity, web
Year
Concept
Type
Fictional brand
Tools
Affinity, Figma, Photoshop, Vizcom

Designed to disconnect.

(OFF)LINE is a fictional brand built around intentional disconnection. In a world designed to keep us online, it makes objects, tools and experiences that invite you to step away from digital noise and get back to your own thoughts.

The identity is restrained and material. A muted editorial palette drawn from ink, paper and stone avoids overstimulation, calm compositions give the eye a rest, and small references to digital systems add a quiet touch of irony.

Typeface Aileron, Light to Bold

Web interface / Designed to be used, not browsed.

GC Estétic.A

Role
Graphic design intern
Year
2026
Type
Rebranding, brand book
Tools
Affinity, Canva, Illustrator, Photoshop

A rebrand for a local beauty salon whose owner wanted a brand that stayed true to who she is.

The answer was elegant femininity: a pastel pink palette, refined typefaces, flower textures and minimal but sophisticated layouts.

The whole system was built in Affinity and Canva on purpose. The brand book had to be practical, so employees with no design background could make new pieces confidently and stay on brand.

Typefaces Tan Pearl, The Seasons, Montserrat
